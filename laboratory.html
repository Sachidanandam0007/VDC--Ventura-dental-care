Smile Lab

in-house ceramics

Where artistry meets precision. Every crown, veneer and restoration is designed and handcrafted in our own lab, shaped to reflect the unique character of each smile and delivered faster, with a perfect fit.

Faster Turnaround: Quicker results without waiting on outside labs.

Seamless Collaboration: Dentists and technicians work side by side.

Higher Precision: Digital tools for a more accurate, natural-looking fit.

Same-Visit Adjustments: Quick refinements, fewer appointments.

Our lab combines advanced digital and hand-finishing tools that enable both precision and artistry. These technologies streamline production while keeping the personal touch that beautiful dentistry requires.

Led by our master ceramist, Daniel Reyes, our lab brings design and craftsmanship under one roof. Dentists and technicians plan every restoration together, from the first digital scan to the final polish.

Each restoration, whether a single crown or a complete smile design, is shaped around your anatomy and the way you smile, speak and express yourself.

Colour layering, translucency and surface texture are tuned by hand so every result mirrors the natural beauty of real teeth.

No outsourcing means better accuracy, shorter turnaround and results that are authentically yours: balanced, functional and beautiful.
